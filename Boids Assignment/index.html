Traffic Boids Simulation

Watch autonomous agents exhibit emergent flocking behavior, mimicking traffic flow patterns.

Mouse Interaction

  • Flee Mode: Cars scatter away from your cursor
  • Follow Mode: Cars are attracted to your cursor

Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Space Pause/Resume simulation
  • R Reset all agents
  • T Cycle through themes
  • B Toggle boundary mode
  • M Toggle mouse follow mode
  • S Toggle settings panel
  • I Toggle this help panel
  • 1-3 Apply presets

Flocking Rules

  • Separation: Avoid crowding neighbors
  • Alignment: Match direction of neighbors
  • Cohesion: Move toward center of group
