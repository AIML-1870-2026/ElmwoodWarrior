Our Vision

One friendly place for both.

We built Habitat Central — a pair of connected web experiences that meet homeowners where they are: learning how to take care of their home, and connecting with the community around them.

Pillar 1

Plain-language guides, seasonal checklists, and a bilingual interface.

Design Principles

How we made our choices

01

Plain over polished

No jargon. No pressure. "Your home, explained in plain steps."

02

Bilingual by default

English and Spanish are equal citizens — one toggle, fully translated.

03

Low-pressure browsing

No accounts, no progress bars, no grades. Explore freely.

04

One unified product

Two experiences, one visual identity, one tab-away from each other.

Reflection

What we learned

Design is the hard part

Once we knew who this was for and what they needed, the code fell into place. The spec was where the thinking happened.

Teamwork + AI = scale

Dividing pages and components let each of us own a piece — then Claude Code helped us stitch a unified product together.

Accessibility pays off

Bilingual support and plain language weren't extras — they were the whole point. The project is better because we led with them.

Real users raise the bar

Knowing a real partner would see this pushed us past "good enough" and into "actually useful."
